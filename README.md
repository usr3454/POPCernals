# CERNALS Popcorn Website

Responsive static website starter with homepage, concept flavour cards, brand story, contact section, privacy policy, terms and conditions, and SVG favicon.

## Before publishing
- Replace `hello@cernals.example` and `@cernals` with real contact details.
- Confirm which flavours are actually sold; the current page labels them as concepts.
- Customise the privacy and terms pages for your actual business practices and legal obligations. These are starter templates, not legal advice.
- The site source does not add fake reviews, fake metrics, customer counters, cursor effects, or scroll animations.

## Preview
Open `index.html` in a browser, or use VS Code Live Server.

## Custom domain
A domain is not registered by this package. Purchase a domain from a registrar, deploy the site to a hosting provider, and configure the provider's DNS and HTTPS instructions. Domain availability is not guaranteed.
